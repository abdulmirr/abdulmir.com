This is what I’m building now.

Favorite is a digital library for your taste. Every book, film and album that shaped you, kept in one place and shared with the people whose taste you trust.

The idea came from a simple observation: the best recommendations I’ve ever gotten came from a friend who knew me, not from an algorithm. Social media rewards attention over intention. Favorite is the quieter alternative, a library you build slowly and a feed made of what your people actually love.

Taste grows socially

You follow your people and see what they save and what they say about it. The feed uses AI to tune daily picks to your taste. Less algorithm slop, more “your sharpest friend told you to watch this.”
