History

I’ve been entrepreneurial my whole life. Ever since moving to America at 10, I helped my Dad sell socks and TVs in flea markets, ornaments in malls, and books on Amazon. I later moved to streetwear, flipping clothes on Grailed until I got banned.

I started my first real company at 15. After teaching myself Photoshop, mass DMing every one of my 300 followers on Instagram, and setting up a Shopify site, I launched my clothing brand Mir Studio. This project continued until I lost the love for it after 3 years. I dropped 10 pieces and made around $25k revenue in total.

In 2024, I moved to New York to study design at Pratt, until I dropped out a semester later. During this time, I made videos & turned Mir into Combat with my best friend. We made products, designed a magazine, and showcased in a pop-up. Combat’s focus has changed since from a clothing brand to a brand providing community/tools for creatives.

Early 2025, I moved back home to Chicago to lock in and start making money. I took a break from all social media and cut off friends. I learned about AI & automation and started a new agency, Built by Abdul. I started off with general make/n8n automation, then focused in B2B cold email.

Start of 2026, I went back to school and fell in love with building/designing software with Claude Code. This is currently what I’m focused on, shipping apps I like and documenting the process on my Instagram & X.
